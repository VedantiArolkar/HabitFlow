Everything a serious habit tracker needs

Every number on screen is computed in Java from rows in MySQL — no mock data, no fake charts.

Live dashboard

Active habits, today's completion rate, current and best streaks, weekly consistency, recent completions and three Chart.js charts — recalculated on every load.

Daily tracking

Tick a habit for any date, add a note, undo it, or step back through history. A database unique key makes double completion impossible.

Honest streaks

Only scheduled days count. Skip a Tuesday that was never scheduled and your streak survives — miss a scheduled day and it resets. Exactly as it should be.

Goals & milestones

Link a goal to habits and progress is pulled from real completions. Milestone messages fire at 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent and are stored as notifications.

Analytics & heatmap

Trend line, per-habit comparison, category performance, a 0-4 level heatmap, best and weakest habits, plus date-range and single-habit filters.

Explainable insights

Rule-based Java logic names your most consistent habit, flags the ones you keep missing and suggests the time of day you actually complete things. No fake AI claims.

From zero to a tracked week

  1. Create your account. Your password is hashed with BCrypt and never stored in plain text.
  2. Add two or three habits. Pick a category, colour, icon, schedule and reminder time.
  3. Tick them off each day. Add a short note if you want context later.
  4. Attach a goal. "Practise Java 40 times" gets a progress bar that fills from real completions.
  5. Read the analytics. Find the weekday you always skip and the habit dragging your average down.
